Search

Find any file, as you type.

Plume keeps a local index of every file and folder in your account, so results appear with each letter, whichever folder they are in. Case and accents don't matter: “resume” finds “Résumé.pdf”.

Your files also show up in Spotlight on iPhone, iPad and Mac: a result opens Plume right on the file.

    How it works

    Sign in once. Then it's just files.

    1. Sign inEnter your server's address and approve Plume in your browser with Nextcloud's Login Flow (two-factor authentication works), or use an app password.
    2. Browse and searchPlume lists your folders and indexes their files, then keeps that mirror fresh from the server.
    3. Use it everywhereOpen files in Finder or the Files app, save into Nextcloud from any app, keep what you need offline.
    Private by design

    Only you and your server.

    Plume has no account of its own, no analytics and no tracking. It talks to your Nextcloud server and nothing else.

    • No third-party servers: every request goes to the Nextcloud server you sign in to.
    • Keychain: the app password is stored in the Keychain.
    • Revoked on sign-out: signing out also revokes the app password on your server.
    • Standard protocols: WebDAV and OCS, as Nextcloud documents them. No server add-on needed.
    • No dependencies: only Apple's frameworks, no third-party code in the app.
    • Open source: GPL-3.0-or-later, every line on GitHub.
