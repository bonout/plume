Nextcloud for iPhone and iPad

Your Nextcloud, native on iPhone.

One screen with your folders, your files and a search field. Plume opens instantly, finds any file as you type, and brings your Nextcloud into the Files app.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later) · iOS 18 and iPadOS 18 or later · not on the App Store yet

What is Plume for iPhone?

Plume is a free, open source Nextcloud client for iPhone and iPad. It is built in SwiftUI with no third-party dependencies, signs in to any Nextcloud server with the Login Flow in your browser (two-factor authentication works) or an app password, and keeps that app password in the Keychain.

The app is a single screen: your folders and files, plus search. Settings are behind the gear button.

Fast, because it's local first

  • Instant folders: Plume keeps a mirror of your folder tree on the device, shows it at once, and refreshes it from the server in the background.
  • Search as you type across your whole account, ignoring case and accents, from a local index of every file and folder.
  • Spotlight: search your Nextcloud files from the Home Screen; a result opens Plume on the file.
  • Server thumbnails for photos and documents, cached on the device.

Part of iOS

  • The Files app: your Nextcloud is a location next to iCloud Drive. Files download when you open them, “Keep Downloaded” keeps them on the device, and edits made in other apps are uploaded.
  • Save from any app: the share sheet, “Save to Files”, “Open in…” or the + button. Uploads never overwrite a file, and large files are sent in resumable chunks.
  • Quick Look for PDFs, images, video, audio and Office documents.
  • Available offline: keep a file or a whole folder on your iPhone.

On iPad

The same app on the bigger screen: drop files from another app into any Plume folder to upload them.

Private by design

Plume talks only to your Nextcloud server. No analytics, no account of ours, no third-party servers. Read the privacy policy.

Limits today

One account at a time, and changes made elsewhere reach the Files app while Plume is open (no background refresh yet). Plume isn't on the App Store yet: build it from source with Xcode.
